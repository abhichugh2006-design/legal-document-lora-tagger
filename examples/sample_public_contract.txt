PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
Riverside County Library District and Northgate Digital Archiving LLC

1. Compensation. The District will pay the Consultant a not-to-exceed sum of $184,000 for the digitization services described in Exhibit A. Invoices shall be submitted quarterly and paid within thirty (30) days of approval by the District's finance office.

2. Early Termination. The District may end this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by giving the Consultant twenty (20) days' written notice. The Consultant shall be paid for work properly completed before the effective date of termination.

3. Protection of Patron Data. The Consultant shall not disclose any library patron names, borrowing histories, or account details to any outside party, and shall restrict access to that information to staff who require it to perform the digitization work.

4. Open Records. The Consultant understands that the District is a public agency subject to the California Public Records Act, and that this Agreement, invoices, and project correspondence may be released to members of the public upon request.

5. Hold Harmless. The Consultant shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the District and its trustees, officers, and employees from any claim, loss, or lawsuit arising from the Consultant's negligent performance of the services.

6. Coverage Requirements. Before beginning work, the Consultant shall provide certificates showing commercial general liability coverage of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, with the District endorsed as an additional insured.

7. Cap on Damages. Neither party will be liable to the other for consequential or incidental damages, and the Consultant's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the total fees paid.

8. Equal Opportunity. In performing this Agreement, the Consultant shall not discriminate against any employee or applicant on the basis of race, religion, sex, national origin, disability, or age.

9. Excused Delays. Neither party is responsible for delays caused by wildfire, flood, earthquake, pandemic, or other events outside its reasonable control, and the project schedule shall be extended for the length of any such delay.

10. Applicable Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, and any lawsuit relating to it shall be filed in the Superior Court for the County of Riverside.
